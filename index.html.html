SELECT HOME WARRANTY — SERVICE PROVIDER AGREEMENT SUMMARY

By submitting this application, the Service Provider ("Contractor") agrees to the following terms and conditions with Select Home Warranty, LLC ("SHW"):


1. Service Standards. Contractor agrees to respond to all work order dispatches within 4 hours during normal business hours and within 24 hours on weekends/holidays.


2. Rates & Billing. Contractor agrees to the discounted SHW rates specified in this application. SHW will pay approved invoices on Net 30 terms (Net 15 for Premium Service Network members).


3. Parts & Materials. Contractor will use OEM or equivalent quality parts. Parts mark-up will not exceed the SHW maximums.


4. Insurance. Contractor must maintain General Liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and list Select Home Warranty, LLC, 1000 Wyckoff Ave, Mahwah, NJ 07430 as certificate holder and additional insured.


5. Licensing. Contractor must maintain all required state and local licenses and permits.


6. Confidentiality. Contractor will not disclose SHW customer information or pricing structures to any third party.


7. Independent Contractor. Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee of SHW.


8. Termination. Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice. SHW may terminate immediately for cause.


9. Dispute Resolution. Disputes will be resolved through binding arbitration in Bergen County, New Jersey.
