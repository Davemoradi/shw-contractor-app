SELECT HOME WARRANTY — SERVICE PROVIDER AGREEMENT SUMMARY

By submitting this application, the Service Provider ("Contractor") agrees to the following terms and conditions with Select Home Warranty, LLC ("SHW"):


1. Service Standards. Contractor agrees to respond to all work order dispatches within 4 hours during normal business hours and within 24 hours on weekends/holidays. Contractor will arrive at the service appointment at the scheduled time and will contact the homeowner if delayed by more than 15 minutes.


2. Rates & Billing. Contractor agrees to the discounted SHW rates specified in this application. Contractor will submit invoices through the SHW portal or email within 48 hours of service completion. SHW will pay approved invoices on Net 30 terms (Net 15 for Premium Service Network members).


3. Parts & Materials. Contractor will use OEM or equivalent quality parts. Parts mark-up will not exceed the SHW maximums listed in this application. If SHW provides parts, Contractor agrees to install them at the labor-only rate.


4. Insurance. Contractor must maintain General Liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and list Select Home Warranty, LLC, 1000 Wyckoff Ave, Mahwah, NJ 07430 as certificate holder and additional insured. Proof of insurance must remain current at all times.


5. Licensing. Contractor must maintain all required state and local licenses and permits. Contractor will notify SHW immediately if any license is suspended, revoked, or expires.


6. Confidentiality. Contractor will not disclose SHW customer information, pricing structures, or business processes to any third party.


7. Independent Contractor. Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee of SHW. Contractor is responsible for all taxes, insurance, and compliance obligations.


8. Termination. Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice. SHW may terminate immediately for cause, including failure to maintain insurance or licensing, poor service quality, or fraud.


9. Dispute Resolution. Disputes will be resolved through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved, disputes will be submitted to binding arbitration in Bergen County, New Jersey.
