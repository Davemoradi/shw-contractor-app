How it works

From sign-up to getting paid, in three steps.

1. Join the network

Fill out the form and we'll call you. Once your license, insurance and W-9 are on file, you're activated for your coverage area.

2. Get dispatched

A homeowner files a claim on a policy they already pay for. If it's your trade and your zip code, the work order comes to your phone. Accept or pass — passing costs nothing.

3. Do the work, get paid

The homeowner pays you their deductible at the visit — $75 to $175 depending on their plan, and owed whether the claim is approved or denied, so you're never eating a wasted trip. That amount is credited against the invoice for that job, and SHW pays you the balance directly. Net 30, or Net 15 on Premium.
