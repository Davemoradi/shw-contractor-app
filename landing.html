Not All Leads Are Equal
A Shared Lead vs. a Real Work Order

Lead marketplaces sell you contact information. We send you a homeowner who already has a claim open and needs the repair now.

Lead Marketplaces

You buy a name and a phone number

Someone filled out a form asking about a price. That same form gets sold to several contractors at once, and whoever calls first usually wins.

  • The same lead is sold to multiple contractors — you're racing everyone else to the phone
  • You pay whether or not they answer, book, or were ever serious
  • Many are still shopping around, comparing prices, or just curious
  • You compete on price against whoever bids lowest
  • Collecting payment is entirely on you
Select Home Warranty

You get a dispatched job with a claim behind it

A homeowner with an active warranty has something broken, has already filed a claim, and is waiting for a technician. That work order is dispatched to you.

  • Dispatched to you — not resold to a list of competitors
  • $0 per lead. You never pay for a name and a phone number
  • Something is already broken and the homeowner wants it fixed now
  • Rates are agreed up front — no bidding war on price
  • SHW pays you directly on approved invoices

The difference is intent. A marketplace lead is someone thinking about a repair. An SHW work order is someone whose water heater failed this morning and has a warranty claim open on it.

FAQ
Common Questions
How quickly will I start getting work orders?
Most contractors start receiving work orders within 1–2 weeks of approval, depending on demand in your coverage area and trade specialty. Premium members get priority routing, which means faster dispatch.
Do I have to pay for leads?
Never. Unlike HomeAdvisor or Angie's List, you never pay per lead. Work orders are dispatched directly to you as part of your membership. The homeowner has already filed a claim — you just show up and do the work.
How do I get paid?
After you complete a work order and submit your invoice, SHW pays you directly. Basic plan members are paid on Net 30 terms. Premium members get Net 15 — meaning you get paid in half the time.
Can I set my own coverage area?
Yes. During the application, you specify the states, counties, and cities you want to cover. We only dispatch jobs within your chosen area. You can update your coverage area at any time.
What trades do you need?
We need contractors across all home service trades: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, appliance repair, garage door, pool/spa, roofing, and more. If you're licensed and insured, we likely have work for you.
Is there a long-term contract?
No. You can cancel your membership at any time with no cancellation fees or penalties. We believe you should stay because it works for your business, not because you're locked into a contract.
What is Dispatch.me?
Dispatch.me is a professional dispatch and scheduling platform included free with every membership. It lets you receive work orders, schedule appointments, track jobs, and manage your workflow — all from your phone. On its own it runs about $300/mo — more than the Basic membership costs in total.
What do I need to apply?
You'll need your business information, contractor license(s), a Certificate of Insurance listing SHW as additional insured, and a completed W-9. The application takes only a few minutes.
